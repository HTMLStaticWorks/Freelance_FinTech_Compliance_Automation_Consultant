International Transfers

Real-Time Transfer Limit & Velocity Rules

Cross-border payments require active monitoring to prevent smurfing (splitting large transfers into small transactions). I construct custom transaction monitors that track sliding-window velocity (e.g. cumulative daily/weekly limits) and automatically apply step-up restrictions or hold transfers for review if suspicious patterns are detected.

Consumer Credit & BNPL

Preventing Automated Loan Fraud

Lending platforms are frequently attacked by botnets generating hundreds of fake profiles. I set up multi-signal defensive structures: analyzing device fingerprinting (hardware hashes, canvas rendering), email address creation age, and proxy/VPN networks. This immediately filters out synthetic identities before they run costly credit queries.

SYNTHETIC IDENTITY DETECT
99.2% Accuracy
